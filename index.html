Find your size

  1. Measure each nailUse a soft ruler or strip of paper. Measure the widest part in millimetres, following the curve.
  2. Match it to the chartIf a nail is within 1 mm of a size, pick that size.
  3. Not sure?Send a photo to or and we will help.

Measurements are nail width in millimetres.

Wear. Care. Repeat.

Reusable means the set keeps its shape and shine if you look after it. Jelly tabs make a set reusable. Glue makes it a one-time wear.

    Remove gently

    Soak fingertips in warm soapy water, then lift each nail from the side.

    Clean and dry

    Wipe the back with an alcohol pad and let it dry fully.

    Store for next time

    Keep the set in its case, away from heat and direct sun.
